Highway To Hell

Completed 10 January 2020

A top-down scrolling game where the player drives along a highway toward hell. The goal is to dodge as many fireballs as possible while fuel slowly drains — collect fuel and repair pickups to stay alive.

Built in about a week using the p5.js library. Note: high scores don't persist after the game is closed.
